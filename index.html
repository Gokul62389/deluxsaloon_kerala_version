About · Playlist

5 Star Thattukada is a small online radio of evergreen Malayalam film songs, the kind you'd hear from the transistor at a roadside thattukada while the tea is being pulled. Open it and the music plays; the tea shop changes with the time in Kerala, from misty mornings and bright afternoons to golden evenings and moonlit nights.
